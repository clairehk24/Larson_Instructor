Coach Education

Instructor Manuscript Status

This is a partial instructor manuscript.

Please select one of the provided debriefing methods from the Introduction. It is important to have a whole-class debriefing session following each simulation.

Simulation Variations and Author Notes

There are different reasons that a coach versus an AT as the EP for this simulation may be preferred. A coach may be more real in these interactions but may ask less direct health care–related questions and may not know when something is incorrect. An AT may not take on the role of a coach well and refuse to participate in the athlete’s care or emulate the other stresses or concerns of a coach. Additionally, an AT may know more than a coach, and this could change the simulation. I have found both to be effective, and it mostly depends on the person. In the past, I have chosen the EP based on my learning goals for that group students.

For this simulation, I always have the students prepare for all three situations and do not tell them their situation until about 24 hours prior to the simulation.
