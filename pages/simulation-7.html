Simulation 7

Phases of Healing

Instructor Simulation Summary

This simulation occurs in a group and involves discussing a patient’s elbow dislocation injury with a coach and explaining the phases of healing to the coach to describe how the athlete will recover.

Instructor-Only Information

This simulation involves talking to a coach who wants to be more involved in a patient’s recovery and is trying to obtain HIPAA information on this patient (their athlete). The students are unaware of this objective by the coach before the simulation. Additionally, the coach will mention some discomfort with the male athletic trainer. While no specific remark has been made and no evidence exists of any inappropriate relationship or contact, the patient is uncomfortable (at least from the coach’s perspective). Regardless, it is assumed that the health care provider (HCP) will follow up with this remark to ensure nothing inappropriate exists. No paperwork exists that provides the coach with permission to access HIPAA information.

Scenario Synopsis

A patient (Emily) is returning to the gym today after an elbow dislocation. The coach wants to speak to the athletic trainers (ATs) about Emily’s expected progress, learn more about the healing process, and understand what can be done for Emily’s training so she can be ready for Olympic Trials in eight weeks.

Content Area

Health care providers (HCPs) with basic knowledge of the healing process, knowledge of health care role and responsibility for patient rehabilitation and care, understanding of coach’s role and responsibility, and basic knowledge of HIPAA as it relates to patients.

Learning Objectives

Explain the phases of healing

Discuss the injury, status, and possible time line for recovery and activity for an elbow dislocation

Adequately address concerns regarding the provision of care

Provide appropriate and necessary information to assist the patient in optimal recovery

Understand and promote the AT’s role in providing health care

Advocate for the patient’s health

Provide necessary and appropriate information to assist in a potential Olympic appearance

Since this simulation is a group simulation, we recommend using the Diamond or the +Delta debriefing models.

Simulation Variations and Author Notes

If instructors do not have the time or resources to incorporate an individual EP encounter for this simulation, it may be run as a group tabletop simulation where the facilitator portrays the coach and students work in groups to formulate responses to the coach’s questions.

This simulation works well as a review or wrap-up discussion following instruction on the phases of healing.

This simulation has been one of the most emotional for students in my experience. They are surprised by the content of the simulation, they are suddenly discussing and conveying information in a group and often don’t know how to do this or to pull aside for private discussion, and they are sometimes emotionally invested in the numerous potential situations presented in this simulation.
