Simulation 6

Dyspnea

Instructor Simulation Summary

Health care providers (HCPs) will assist an athlete who is experiencing symptoms of dyspnea and is presenting consistent with one of the following conditions: asthma, anxiety, arrhythmia, or anaphylaxis.

Scenario Synopsis

An athlete is experiencing difficulty breathing and other symptoms that make a specific diagnosis difficult without more investigation. You will assess the patient to determine the condition they are experiencing and provide treatment or referral as appropriate.

Content Area

Students must be familiar with dyspneic conditions and causes and be able to perform tests or assessments that may rule in or out conditions related to dyspnea.

Learning Objectives

Differentially diagnose and prioritize conditions that may present similarly or share symptomology

Select specific tests (vitals, spirometry, etc.) to assess for specific dyspneic conditions

Use pertinent negative patterns to help arrive at a clinical decision

Identify the affected body systems to refine a differential diagnosis

Manage the identified emergent condition

Appropriately refer or plan next steps for each patient

HCP Prebrief

Within eight minutes, you are tasked with assessing and treating all conditions found.

When you have successfully addressed the injury and treated the patient, you can leave; you do not need to use all eight minutes. If you run out of time, you will be told, “Time is up.” You must stop where you are and leave the simulation area.

You may be asked to answer clarifying questions. This should not be interpreted as poor performance. Clarifying questions may be asked for many reasons.

Please return your equipment to the starting area where you received it.

Please ensure all equipment is returned to its original state or replaced, if needed.

Treat this person as a real patient. You should provide care as you would in an actual situation. If you are unclear, you can always ask the proctor.

If there is a piece of equipment that you need and it is not available, you can verbalize that piece of equipment and its use.

SAFETY: If any person says “Time-out; time-out,” please stop actions immediately and as safely as possible. Unless you can see that the SP needs assistance, you should immediately leave the area and find a proctor.

If you have any questions, please speak to your instructor.

Debrief

Please select one of the provided debriefing methods from the Introduction. It is important to have a whole-class debriefing session following each simulation.

This is a complex simulation with up to four different scenarios. If a debrief occurs with all students who had different patients, it may be prolonged.

Simulation Variations and Author Notes

This simulation could be used to practice medication administration. An HCP provider could practice albuterol (nebulized or MDI), epinephrine from a vial or autoinjector, diphenhydramine by tablet or vial, and fluid administration. This would address CAATE Standards 74 and 75.

If you choose to administer any medications as part of the simulation, remember to discuss the appropriate rules for administration: when, how, why—drug, dose, route, patient, route, time. If a person’s prescription was used, remember to discuss the decision to use it and the process by which the decision was made.
