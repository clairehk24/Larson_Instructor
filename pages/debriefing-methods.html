Debriefing Methods

NAME METHOD PURPOSE EXAMPLE SIMULATION
Diamond1 Use a structural visual reminder (Diamond) to guide debrief. The top of the diamond is Describe, the middle is Analysis, and the bottom is Application. There are specific questions and prompts that keep the discussion focused on the experience more than technical aspects. Exertional Heat Stroke (Simulation 23)
Gather, analyze, summarize (GAS)2 Involves three phases where a facilitator asks prompting questions. This debriefing method is designed to outline what happened in the simulation, self-assess, and plan for the future. Foot Pain Telehealth (Simulation 25)
Plus-Delta (+Delta)3 What went well, what can be changed. Allows students to pick focus of discussion and reframe their experience. Time-Out Ankle (Simulation 1)
Reflective practice in health care4 Students reflect on a series of questions. Allows students to self-assess and reflect on their performance as they look forward to future experiences. Snowboard Accident (Simulation 19)
PEARLS debriefing framework5 Involves a five-phase, semi-structured approach to debriefing. This method allows instructors to set debriefing ground rules, gather students’ reactions, and then analyze the components of the simulation. Taping Ethics (Simulation 14)
Trauma-informed psychologically safe (TiPS) debriefing framework26 Involves a five-phase, semi-structured approach to supporting student’s emotional needs through a debrief following traumatic simulations. This debriefing method focuses on emotions and psychological stressors students experienced during a simulation. Emergency Action Plan (EAP) (Simulation 3)
