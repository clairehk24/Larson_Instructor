Simulation 5

Simulation 5: Special Test Roulette: Upper Extremity

Instructor Simulation Summary

Students will research and serve as a patient and proctor of a specific injury requiring a special test. Then, as a health care provider (HCP), they will complete five stations with a brief scenario at each station requiring the HCP to decide on a special test to diagnose the injury. At each station, the HCP will perform the special test, answer patient and proctor questions, and then move to the next scheduled location.

Instructor-Only Information

If you are going to use the suggested format of six stations, be sure to assign Presimulation Activity 6 Special Test Selection to students before the simulation. Each student should receive the Information for Station for the station for which they will be serving as the patient and proctor.

Scenario Synopsis

Six stations will be set up for this simulation. During the simulation, students will be patient and proctor for each other while other students are the HCPs. HCPs will be divided into groups of two and assigned one station for which they will be a patient and proctor. During the first portion of the simulation, HCP 1 will be the patient and proctor, and then during the second portion of the simulation, HCP 2 will be the patient and proctor. The situation will not change, just the person playing the role of the patient and proctor. The pair will be assigned an injury and must learn and know the relevant special tests for this injury, then take turns acting as the patient and proctor during their rotation. Students will rotate through the remaining five stations for which they are not assigned as the patient and proctor. Each station will have an examination table. No special tools or equipment will be needed for the students to complete these simulations.

Content Area

Students must be familiar with selecting and using special tests for upper-extremity orthopedic examination and diagnosis.

Learning Objectives

Correctly identify special tests appropriate to assess a differential diagnosis

Correctly perform each chosen special test

Interpret the results of each special test to correctly diagnose the injury

CAATE 2020 Standards

Standard 71

Please select one of the provided debriefing methods from the Introduction. It is important to have a whole-class debriefing session following each simulation.

This simulation involves multiple scenarios with brief opportunities for clinical decision making. As such, the Diamond debriefing method may be useful to articulate and address any clinical and application-based questions.

Simulation Variations and Author Notes

This simulation could be modified to include any special tests students learn in their evaluation courses. This simulation could also be used as a clinical education readiness tool or to practice for a clinical rotation prior to the students’ beginning the rotation. You may also choose to have students work in pairs and alternate who is the HCP and patient, and then each complete three of the prescribed stations.

This roulette simulation has been coordinated with various providers previously. Using actual providers from a particular setting or a variety of settings is very informative for students. For instance, we have used clinical personnel and field management athletic trainers (ATs), and each person performs tests differently based on time-lapse, clinical rules, location, and hand size, and so on. If you use these clinicians as the patient and proctor, you may consider having a short debrief immediately after the simulation to allow these providers to discuss both their differences and logic and their work settings.
